# Prompt: Replace Service Icons with Realistic Indian AI Images

Replace all existing service icons/symbols in the **"Our Services"** section with **realistic AI-generated photographs** that have a clear **Indian context**.

## Overall Style

The images should feel like they belong to a modern Indian home services marketplace (similar to Urban Company) and immediately build trust.

### Image Requirements

* Remove all icons completely.
* Generate **photorealistic AI images**, not illustrations, vectors, clipart, cartoons, or 3D renders.
* Every image should feature **Indian service professionals**.
* Professionals should have an authentic Indian appearance with natural Indian skin tones and facial features.
* Clothing should resemble what local Indian technicians actually wear—clean uniforms, polo shirts, work shirts, aprons, safety gloves, and tool belts where appropriate. Avoid overly corporate or Western-looking uniforms.
* Use modern Indian homes and apartments as the background.
* Images should be **slightly close-up (waist-up or upper-body framing)** so the worker and their tools are clearly visible while still showing enough of the work environment. Avoid extreme close-ups of faces or wide shots where the worker appears tiny.
* Subject should occupy roughly **60–70% of the frame**.
* Natural lighting.
* Warm and welcoming color tones.
* Consistent photography style across all categories.
* Premium, trustworthy, and professional look.
